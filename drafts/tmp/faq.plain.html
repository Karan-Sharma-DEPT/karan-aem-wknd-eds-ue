Everything you need to know.

How does Rubrik protect against ransomware?

Backups are written once and never altered, so an attacker who reaches your production estate still cannot reach the copies. Anomaly detection flags unusual encryption activity as it happens, and recovery points are scanned before they are restored.

What cloud platforms does Rubrik support?

AWS, Azure and Google Cloud are supported natively, alongside the major on-premises hypervisors. Read the full platform matrix for version-level detail.

How quickly can we recover after a cyberattack?

Recovery is measured in minutes rather than days. The platform keeps every restore point live, so there is no rehydration step between choosing a point in time and bringing the workload back.

Is Rubrik compatible with our existing backup infrastructure?

Yes. Existing catalogues can be ingested in place, which means the migration does not require a parallel run of two products.

What compliance frameworks does Rubrik support?

Reporting templates ship for the frameworks most customers are audited against:

  • SOC 2 Type II
  • ISO 27001
  • HIPAA and GDPR
How does Rubrik handle data sovereignty and residency?

Policies are bound to a region at the point the data is written, so a workload governed by a residency rule cannot be replicated outside its permitted geography even by an administrator.
